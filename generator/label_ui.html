    How to label

    For each household, list every item the preparer should ask the client for, for the 2026 federal return only. Use whatever short name you would put on a request list, one item per row, and put how many in the count box. Leave out what every client brings, such as a photo ID or bank details, and write none if the preparer should ask for nothing.

    Everything saves as you type, to . The rules' answers stay hidden until every household has a label and you choose to compare.

    Enter adds a row. Backspace in an empty row removes it. goes to the next household.

    Not tax advice. All households are synthetic.
